# Lightbeam

> Lightbeam is an AI data security and guardrails platform. It discovers and classifies
> sensitive data across cloud, SaaS and on-premises systems, ties that data to identity

## Platform capabilities

- AI Security — detects shadow AI use, inspects prompts and uploaded files, and blocks
  sensitive data from reaching unauthorized GenAI platforms.
- Data Security Posture Management (DSPM) — discovers sensitive data and its exposure
  across cloud, SaaS and on-premises environments.
- Data Classification — classifies and labels sensitive data, including distinguishing
  real from synthetic data.
- Risk Scoring — prioritizes risk by sensitivity, access pattern, destination, identity
  and business impact.
- Automated Remediation — blocks, alerts, quarantines or minimizes risky exposure.
- Access Governance — controls which users, applications, copilots and agents can reach
  sensitive data.
- Privacy at Scale — connects data usage to privacy obligations, retention policy and
  audit requirements.
- Data Retention — removes unnecessary or risky data.
- Breach & Ransomware Protection — limits incident blast radius.
- User Entity Behavior Analytics (UEBA) — detects anomalous behavior by users, agents
  and applications around sensitive data.
- Data Identity Graph — the underlying model connecting sensitive data to identity,
  access context, business requirement and AI usage.
